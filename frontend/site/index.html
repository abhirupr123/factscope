What it helps with

Investigate faster while keeping your judgment.

01

Break a page into claims

FactScope extracts specific statements that can be investigated instead of reducing an entire article to one score.

02

Find reporting and evidence

See direct fact-checks, supporting or contradicting evidence, matching reports, and broader context under the claim they relate to.

03

Check images in context

Right-click an image to review manipulation indicators, caption consistency, visible source clues, and uncertainty separately.

04

Share a verification snapshot

Create a public result page that preserves the analysis and its caveats without requiring the recipient to install the extension.

How it works

You choose what leaves the page.

FactScope has no persistent access to every site you visit. It receives content only after an explicit scan or image-verification action and your first-scan disclosure.
