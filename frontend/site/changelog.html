Version 1.5.0 — Evidence-first experience

August 15, 2026
  • First-run onboarding explains what is transmitted and that FactScope assists verification rather than determining truth.
  • Claims and evidence links now appear before community controls.
  • Offline, cold-start, timeout, provider, unsupported-page, blocked-image, and no-checkable-claim states have distinct guidance.
  • Article results use plain-language coverage labels and a compact evidence overview.
  • Image results separate visual manipulation, caption consistency, and visible source clues.
  • Paid-tier and license controls remain hidden during the free beta.

Version 1.4.1

August 2026
  • Migrated the extension to versioned evidence responses.
  • Improved display of matching reporting and broader context for low-reported claims.

  • Updated production permissions to use user-triggered page access.
