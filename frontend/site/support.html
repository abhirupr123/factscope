Frequently asked questions

Why does FactScope show coverage but no verdict?

Matching reporting can help investigate a claim without confirming every exact detail. FactScope reserves supported or contradicted findings for stronger, inspectable evidence.

Why did the same article take time again?

A cache can miss when the extracted content, canonical URL, or analysis version changed, or when the stored result expired. A genuine cache hit should return quickly and should not reduce the daily quota.

Does “no external coverage found” mean false?

No. It means FactScope did not find qualifying coverage during that scan. New, local, paywalled, or poorly indexed reporting may be missing.

Can I remove my data?

Yes. Open Privacy & data in the extension to clear local history, disable optional telemetry, or delete installation-linked server data. See the privacy policy for temporary abuse-prevention records.

Should I send the article text to support?

No. Start with the error label, request ID, extension version, and whether it was a page or image scan. Do not send private or sensitive content.

Contact support

Include the extension version, approximate time of the scan, error label, and request ID if one was shown. Support is currently handled by the project maintainer, so replies are not guaranteed to be immediate.
