FactScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

FactScope does not collect personal information. No accounts, no emails, no tracking.

What FactScope Does

FactScope is a browser extension that analyzes web content for misinformation signals. When you scan a page or image, the extension sends the page content to our backend for AI analysis.

Data We Collect

  • Page content you scan — the URL, title, and text of pages you explicitly choose to analyze. We only process content when you click "Scan" or "Verify image".
  • Anonymous user ID — a random UUID generated locally on your device. This is not linked to your identity, browser, or any personal information. It's used solely to prevent duplicate community flags.
  • Scan results — AI-generated trust scores, verdicts, and evidence for scanned content. These are stored to enable the community verification feature.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Browsing history or pages you visit without scanning
  • Personal information (name, email, location, IP address)
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Data from other browser extensions
  • Keystrokes or form data

How Data Is Used

  • AI Analysis: Scanned content is sent to our backend, which uses AI models to assess credibility. The content is not stored beyond caching for performance.
  • Community Features: Scan results are shared anonymously so other users can see if content was previously verified or flagged.
  • Caching: Results are cached to avoid redundant analysis. Cached entries expire after 24 hours.

Third-Party Services

FactScope uses the following third-party services to perform analysis:

  • Google Gemini / Gemma AI — for content and image analysis
  • Google Fact Check Tools API — for checking existing fact-checks
  • Google News RSS — for news corroboration
  • Turso (libSQL) — for anonymous result storage

No personal data is shared with these services. Only the page content you explicitly scan is sent for analysis.

Data Storage & Security

Scan results are stored in a cloud database (Turso) with no personal identifiers. The anonymous user ID stored on your device can be reset at any time by clearing the extension's local storage.

Your Rights

  • You can stop using FactScope at any time by uninstalling the extension.
  • You can clear your anonymous ID by going to Chrome Settings > Extensions > FactScope > Clear data.
  • FactScope never scans anything without your explicit action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
